# German Language Study Hub (Deutsch Lernen)

Central repository for German language acquisition, textbooks, custom-crafted study materials, exam prep, and academic/professional German for OVGU and career development.

---

## Directory Structure

```text
German/
├── textbooks/                      # Downloaded and reference textbooks
│   ├── coursebooks/                # General Lehr- und Arbeitsbücher (Netzwerk, Menschen, Aspekte, etc.)
│   ├── grammar/                    # Dedicated grammar books (Grammatik aktiv, Hammer's, etc.)
│   └── vocabulary/                 # Vocabulary references (Wortschatz & Grammatik, B1/B2 Wortschatz)
│
├── created-material/               # AI-generated and custom study notes & summaries
│   ├── grammar-notes/              # Deep-dive explanations, rule summaries, and syntax trees
│   ├── vocabulary-lists/           # Thematic vocabulary, frequency lists, and flashcard exports
│   ├── cheat-sheets/               # Quick reference tables (cases, prepositions, verb conjugations)
│   └── exercises/                  # Targeted practice worksheets and solved problem sets
│
├── exam-prep/                      # Standardized certification prep
│   ├── model-tests/                # Practice papers for Goethe-Zertifikat / Telc / TestDaF / DSH
│   └── strategies/                 # Exam tips, time management, scoring criteria
│
├── academic-and-professional/      # Targeted German for university and work in Germany
│   ├── email-templates/            # Formal templates for professors, HiWi outreach, and admin
│   └── medical-engineering-german/ # Specialized MedTech, clinical engineering, and device terminology
│
└── audio-and-media/                # Audio scripts, listening comprehension files, podcast transcripts
```

## CEFR Learning Roadmap

| Level | Focus | Primary Goal | Recommended Core Books |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A1 / A2** | Foundations (Grammatik Basics, Satzbau, Daily Vocabulary) | Basic conversation, everyday life in Magdeburg, basic emails | *Netzwerk neu A1/A2*, *Grammatik aktiv A1–B1* |
| **B1** | Independence (Nebensätze, Passiv, Konjunktiv II, Expanded Wortschatz) | Fluency in everyday discussions, official administrative communication | *Aspekte neu B1+ / Sicher B1+*, *Grammatik aktiv A1–B1* |
| **B2** | Professional Competence (Nomen-Verb-Verbindungen, Partizipialattribute) | Technical/engineering discussions, HiWi roles, job interviews | *Aspekte neu B2*, *Grammatik aktiv B2–C1*, *DSH/TestDaF Training* |
| **C1** | Academic & Scientific Mastery | Thesis writing in German, academic presentations, research papers | *Erkundungen C1*, *Wissenschaftssprache Deutsch* |

## Workflow & Next Steps

1. **Textbooks & References**: Place PDF copies of your chosen textbooks into `textbooks/coursebooks/`, `textbooks/grammar/`, and `textbooks/vocabulary/`.
2. **Material Generation**: As we study specific topics (e.g. Adjektivdeklination, Relativsätze, Passive voice in engineering contexts), summaries and cheat sheets will be placed in `created-material/`.
3. **Professional Application**: Use `academic-and-professional/email-templates/` for formal university outreach and `medical-engineering-german/` for domain-specific terminology.
